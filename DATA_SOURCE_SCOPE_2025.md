# Frozen IRENA 2025 source-unit and coverage declaration

**Canonical manuscript:** *Renewable Energy Public Finance: Financial Development, Institutions, and Income Groups*

**Source and update date:** IRENASTAT archived public-finance matrix **PUBFIN_2025_H2_PX**; last updated **23 July 2025**. [Official 2025 table](https://pxweb.irena.org/pxweb/en/IRENASTAT/IRENASTAT__Finance/PUBFIN_2025_H2_PX.px/).

**Recorded concept:** commitments by recipient country for selected public renewable-energy finance, aggregated by recipient country and renewable technology. This is not a census of public plus private national capital expenditure, annual disbursements, or verified project implementation.

**Operational published unit:** `million USD at 2022 prices and exchange rates (using donor country deflators)`. This is explicit in the contemporaneous 2025 PxWeb table's information panel and footnotes. The **2025 yearbook's generic introductory table notes** separately refer to `current prices` and integer rounding, contrary to table labels and online series-specific metadata. For the frozen 2025 finance table, this project follows the more specific, dated IRENASTAT metadata and does not apply an additional deflator or impute alternative units. The competing generic note remains documented, **not** reinterpreted as a second price conversion.

**Primary reporting sources:** OECD DAC Statistics database (2024) and IRENA Public Finance Database (2025), as identified by the official 2025 IRENASTAT finance table. The [IRENA Renewable Energy Statistics 2025 annual publication](https://www.irena.org/Publications/2025/Jul/Renewable-energy-statistics-2025) describes finance data spanning 2014–2023, from OECD DAC and **16 major multilateral, bilateral and national development-finance institutions**. Earlier IRENA documentation has referred to 20 institutions. Neither headline count identifies the complete roster and eligibility rules for each historical observation year. No provider-year or transaction-level roster is available in the consulted published tables; it is **not asserted** that all providers participated in all years.

**Separate 2026 comparison:** [IRENASTAT PUBFIN_2026_H2_PX](https://pxweb.irena.org/pxweb/en/IRENASTAT/IRENASTAT__Finance/PUBFIN_2026_H2_PX.px/), updated **28 September 2026**, identifies `million USD at 2023 prices and exchange rates`, with DAC 2025 and IRENA Public Finance Database 2026 as sources. The 2025 and 2026 series have different reporting coverage and native price years. They were used only in distinct vintage-specific descriptive and matched-sample estimates; they were **not** spliced or treated as directly comparable dollar amounts.

**Record-missingness rule:** an unpublished cell is missing/ineligible in a selected source; it is not zero national finance. Published numeric zeros are separately retained.

**Reproducibility and rights:** Historical author-frozen inputs and source downloads are not public in this repository while source-specific/third-party redistribution rights are examined. The dated IRENASTAT metadata establishes the *unit* but does not provide complete transaction reconstruction or blanket reuse permission for all mixed files. The existing public verification scripts validate aggregates; end-to-end raw-to-panel replication requires lawful access to the matching frozen inputs.

**External confirmation still desirable (not presumed):** a written response from IRENA on (i) its contradictory generic table note, (ii) providers and methodology per year, (iii) historical revisions/cancellations and the bridge to the 2026 database, and (iv) derivative data redistribution terms. No communication with IRENA has been sent as part of this update.

**Data source attribution:** International Renewable Energy Agency (IRENA), *Renewable Energy Statistics 2025*, © IRENA 2025, and contemporaneous IRENASTAT matrix PUBFIN_2025_H2_PX. In metadata citations, the table-specific published unit takes precedence over a generic yearbook boilerplate note.
