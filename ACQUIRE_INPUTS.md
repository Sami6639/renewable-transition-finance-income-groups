## Source acquisition and definitions

- IRENA Renewable Energy Statistics 2025: https://www.irena.org/Publications/2025/Jul/Renewable-energy-statistics-2025 . Use the 2014–2023 recipient-country public-financing tables from this vintage. Preserve missing records, published numeric zeros and the table-specific provisional constant-2022-USD basis.
- World Bank WDI: acquire the exact indicator vintages corresponding to the frozen panel, including FD.AST.PRVT.GD.ZS (bank private credit/GDP), constant-2015-USD GDP per capita, population, trade/GDP and CPI inflation. Historical World Bank income classifications and WGI governance estimates are also needed. Indicator identifiers and variable definitions are retained in code; use the institutional data portals and their metadata rather than invented or interpolated observations.
- IMF overall Financial Development index: recover the author-retained original export; its coverage in this study ends in 2020. No later values may be imputed. Check the applicable source terms for the exact export.
- World Bank RISE: https://rise.esmap.org/methodology . The original input is the WB_RISE Data360 download (dataset 2025-04-01 / series 2025-04-07 metadata), with five renewable-energy pillar indicators and their total. It is used to validate retrospective scores, not as an exogenous instrument. Source metadata in the original archive identifies CC BY 4.0, with attribution and an indicator-level dispute-resolution addition. This repository distributes no RISE data dump.
